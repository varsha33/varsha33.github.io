          <div class="varsha-about-text">
            <p>I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Multimodal Language Processing Group at the <a href="https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/">Max Planck Institute for Informatics</a>, led by <a href="https://www.uni-saarland.de/lehrstuhl/demberg.html">Prof. Dr. Vera Demberg</a>. I am also associated with the Department of Language Science and Technology at Saarland University and the <a href="https://www.neuroexplicit.org/">Research Training Group on Neuro-Explicit Models of Language, Vision, and Action</a> at Saarland University.</p>
            <p>My research goes beyond the transcript. Human communication is richly multimodal: meaning is not just spoken but gestured, timed, and implied through tone, gaze, and expression. I build language and vision-language models that can read this full signal, picking up on co-speech gestures, turn-taking cues, sarcasm, and emotional subtext, and generate responses that are not just fluent but socially and semantically grounded.</p>
            <p class="varsha-subtle">My work spans three directions:</p>
            <ul>
              <li><strong>Multimodal comprehension:</strong> using co-speech gestures to improve discourse and turn-taking modelling in language models, and generating semantically meaningful gestures.</li>
              <li><strong>Multimodal pragmatics and reasoning:</strong> probing how vision-language models handle sarcasm, irony, implicit harm, and other pragmatic phenomena, and diagnosing where and why they fall short.</li>
              <li><strong>Human-centred applications:</strong> from gesture coaching for public speaking to affective computing and fair facial expression recognition.</li>
            </ul>
